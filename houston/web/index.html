                Siri, Google & shortcuts

                Give a shortcut on your phone a key so it can ask Houston for status or switch modes, without opening the app. On iPhone that lets you say “Hey Siri, Apollo status”.

                Key (send it as Authorization: Bearer …) Status, read aloud (GET) Actions (POST): start, stop, eco, balanced, turbo, node-start, node-stop, super_eco, custom-14
